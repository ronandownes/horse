A quick note for the Ryan family.

Hi Tomas and family,

This is a demo only, prepared to show what a modern Tipperary Equestrian Centre website could look and feel like if you are still interested.

The practical information — lessons, showjumping, pony camps, trekking, livery, facilities, events, arena hire and contact details — can sit alongside a stronger educational identity, so a visitor begins to understand the coaching philosophy before they arrive.

The brochure material has been paraphrased and expanded for the web, and the coaching ideas from the earlier email have been developed into rider-learning content. The photographs shown include AI-assisted concept images based on the indoor-arena image supplied; they would be replaced or approved before a finished site is presented as official.

If you like the direction, the easiest next step is one consolidated email confirming what should stay, what should change, which services are current, and which photographs / contact details you want used.

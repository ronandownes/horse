TRAINING

Showjumping

Develop the quality of the ride before increasing the height or complexity of the fence.

01

Rhythm

Maintain a regular, balanced pace that gives the horse a predictable approach.

02

Poles

Use ground poles and simple exercises to develop straightness, line and rider position.

03

Small fences

Keep the rider balanced and the horse confident while adding jumping technique.

04

Courses

Combine turns, related distances, lines, rhythm and decision-making around a complete track.

A centre with showjumping history

The Ryan family material identifies showjumping as an important part of the centre's history and describes leagues during the year.

What the rider is learning

  • Approach a fence in balance rather than chasing for speed.
  • Keep a consistent line and rhythm.
  • Use eyes, body and leg to organise turns.
  • Recover rhythm after each fence.
  • Review the round and make one or two specific changes next time.

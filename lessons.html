RIDING

Riding lessons

A clear progression from confidence and basic control to balance, rhythm, canter, polework and more advanced riding.

Children & adults

The centre's own material describes riding lessons for children and adults. A useful first conversation is about age, previous experience, confidence, goals and the type of riding the person wants to develop.

01

Foundation

Safe mounting, balanced position, walk, halt, steering and basic rising trot.

02

Control

Clear leg aids, circles, transitions, rhythm and riding accurate lines.

03

Balance

Canter, steadier contact, independent seat, polework and better body control.

04

Partnership

Feel, timing, course riding, more technical exercises and confident decision-making.

Before your first lesson

  • Tell the centre your riding experience honestly — complete beginner is useful information.
  • Ask what footwear, helmet and clothing are required.
  • Arrive early enough for instructions and horse allocation.
  • Tell the instructor about anything relevant to comfort or confidence.
  • Expect horse welfare and safe handling to come before speed or difficulty.

YOUNG RIDERS

Pony camps & group riding

A camp can be more than extra saddle time: riding, horse care, stable management and confidence can all sit together.

Riding

Mounted sessions matched to the group's level, with clear goals such as position, steering, transitions, poles or jumping.

Horse care

Grooming, safe approach, tack awareness and everyday care help riders understand the horse beyond the lesson.

Stable skills

Practical yard routines can build independence, responsibility and respect for horses and equipment.

Confidence

Group activities create a social, supportive way to practise new skills and celebrate progress.

Information parents should be able to find

A finished camp page should publish dates, age guidance, riding level, start and finish times, clothing, lunch arrangements, price, cancellation policy and exactly how booking works. Until the centre confirms those details, this demo deliberately does not invent them.

Residential riding

The centre's earlier brochure also referred to residential riding holidays. That is a distinctive service worth retaining on the site only if the Ryan family confirms it is still offered.

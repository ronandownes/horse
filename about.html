OUR STORY

About the centre

A family equestrian centre at Mill Road in Thurles, with a long connection to riding, showing, jumping and horse care.

Established in 1985

The centre's original material records that Tipperary Equestrian Centre was established by the Ryan family in 1985, growing from a long family involvement with horses and ponies.

That history gives the website a better starting point than generic marketing: a working, family-run place where riders learn and different parts of the equestrian community meet.

Mill Road, Thurles

The centre is based at Mill Road, Thurles, Co. Tipperary, E41 WF10. Its published facilities include indoor and outdoor riding areas, cross-country, trekking or trail riding, livery and supporting centre spaces.

A teaching identity

The site does more than list services. It explains what riders are learning, why position matters, how aids become clearer and how confidence develops through sensible progression. That is why Coaching has its own page rather than being buried inside Lessons.

What the client can shape

This first full version gives each major area its own page. The Ryan family can now decide which pages deserve more detail, which services are current, which should be removed and where their own photographs, staff profiles, schedules and booking information should be added.

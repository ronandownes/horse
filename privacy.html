Demo privacy note

Privacy & cookies.

This demonstration site does not collect, submit or store personal information.

The enquiry form and availability calendar are visual demonstrations only. A finished website would include an appropriate privacy notice explaining what information is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained and who can access it.

The demo does not intentionally use analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking cookies. If analytics, embedded services or other non-essential technologies are added later, the live site should use a suitable consent mechanism.

Business contact details, social-media links and booking integrations should be confirmed by the centre before launch.
